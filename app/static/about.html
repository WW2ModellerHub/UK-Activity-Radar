About the radar

Live UK transport and activity on one map

UK Activity Radar brings together live trains, buses, aircraft, road incidents and traffic so you can understand what is happening across Britain without switching between several services.

The radar is an independent information service. Live feeds can be delayed, incomplete or unavailable and must not be used for safety-critical or emergency decisions.

Live trains and station boards

View reported train movements, operators and routes when the railway feeds can identify them. Between sparse reports, clearly labelled estimates follow mapped timetable timing points rather than pretending to be GPS. Search for a station to open its departures and select a service to inspect its calling points.

Live buses and nearby stops

Show live bus positions from the Bus Open Data Service, or use your location to find the nearest fixed bus stop and its available departure predictions.

Aircraft across the UK

Explore aircraft positions received by the configured ADS-B feed. Aircraft are grouped into recognisable map symbols such as airliners, propeller aircraft, fast jets, heavy transports and helicopters.

Roads, incidents and traffic

Selected official and licensed feeds provide road incidents, works, cameras and traffic context. Availability varies by provider and Radar+ access.

Discover unusual live activity

Discover highlights unusual or special-interest movements that the source data can support. Labels describe evidence from the feeds and avoid claiming an identity that has not been published.

Privacy by design

The free map does not require an account. Saved places and discoveries are kept in your browser unless a feature clearly says otherwise. Optional Google Analytics is not loaded until you choose to allow it.
