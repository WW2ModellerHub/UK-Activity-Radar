Data licences and attribution

UK Activity Radar combines independent public and licensed feeds. It is not an official transport, emergency or navigation service. Provider names identify data sources and do not imply endorsement.

Live data may be delayed, incomplete or wrong. Do not use this site for safety-critical, emergency, operational or investment decisions.

Rail statistics

Station passenger numbers and official punctuality and cancellation figures are from the Office of Rail and Road (Estimates of station usage, table 1415; passenger rail performance, tables 3138 and 3124), used under the Open Government Licence v3.0.

UK Train Dataset

Exact-number rolling-stock reference fields are derived from Jamie Lord’s UK Train Dataset under the MIT licence. The dataset includes historic and private vehicles and may be incomplete or out of date; UK Activity Radar never infers a class from a service headcode.

EUMETNET OPERA rain radar

Rain radar from the EUMETNET OPERA weather radar network composite, licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0). The composite combines national weather radars, including those of the Met Office and Met Éireann. The radar reprojects it to the map, cuts it to the UK and Ireland and colours it by rain rate.

Wikimedia Commons train and vessel images

Optional train and vessel reference images only appear when the object has enough identity for a defensible match. Every displayed image links to its file page and shows the creator/source and individual commercial-reuse licence supplied by Wikimedia Commons. Aircraft photos do not use Wikimedia.

Planespotters.net aircraft thumbnails

For an aircraft with a valid ICAO address, Radar may show a linked thumbnail returned by the public Planespotters Photo API. The thumbnail links to its original photo page and keeps the photographer credit visible. Radar does not copy or republish the full photograph or the Planespotters aircraft database.
