Data licences and attribution

UK Activity Radar combines independent public and licensed feeds. It is not an official transport, emergency or navigation service. Provider names identify data sources and do not imply endorsement.

Live data may be delayed, incomplete or wrong. Do not use this site for safety-critical, emergency, operational or investment decisions.

UK Train Dataset

Exact-number rolling-stock reference fields are derived from Jamie Lord’s UK Train Dataset under the MIT licence. The dataset includes historic and private vehicles and may be incomplete or out of date; UK Activity Radar never infers a class from a service headcode.

Wikimedia Commons images

Images are optional and only appear when the live object has enough identity for a defensible match. Every displayed image links to its file page and shows the creator/source and the individual commercial-reuse licence supplied by Wikimedia Commons. No image is presented as a photograph of the live object unless its identity matches.
