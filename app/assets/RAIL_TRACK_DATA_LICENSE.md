# Railway track geometry attribution

`rail_track_geometry.geojson` is adapted from the `track only.geojson`
dataset in [Raileasy Ltd's railway-gis-data project](https://github.com/raileasyuk/railway-gis-data).

The source combines Network Rail reference-line geometry released under the
Open Government Licence 3.0 with TIPLOC work from GB Railway Data Ltd. The
combined dataset is licensed by Raileasy Ltd under
[CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

This copy is used as a connected routing graph for passenger-facing live train
positions. No endorsement by Network Rail, Raileasy Ltd, or GB Railway Data Ltd
is implied.
